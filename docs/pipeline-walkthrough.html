Hirobius · delivery pipeline

Lead to invoice, end‑to‑end — and where it breaks

Every stage of lead → score → site → preview → outreach → reply → deal → invoice, its moving parts, and an honest status. Built for the operator, not the archive.

The headline

Both ends are built — sourcing and scoring are live, outreach is scaffolded. But the middle is the broken link: the site actually gets generated (③) and published (④) has never run end-to-end. Until it does, there is no preview link to send. That middle — not the outreach engine — is the #1 build target.
